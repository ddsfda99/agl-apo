## Expected behavior
Given:
```python

```

- Default strict behavior unchanged:

- New behavior:
  - `from_wkt(bad, fix_structure=True)` loads successfully and returns a polygon whose exterior ring is closed (`coords[0] == coords[-1]`).
  - `import shapely.wkt as wkt; wkt.loads(bad, fix_structure=True)` also works (but **do not** modify `shapely/__init__.py` to attach `wkt`).
