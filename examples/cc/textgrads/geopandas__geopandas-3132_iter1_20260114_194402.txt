Design considerations and correctness
- Adding a public to_geo that delegates to the private _to_geo is the safest change: users get the dict they want, internal behavior remains unchanged, and to_json continues to add CRS when appropriate.
- Keeping CRS logic out of to_geo is correct, since the dict representation returned by __geo_interface__ and _to_geo does not include CRS; only to_json injects CRS for non-WGS84.
- The signature to_geo(self, na="null", show_bbox=False, drop_id=False) matches the parameters used by _to_geo and gives users the control that __geo_interface__ (property) does not.
- The tests wisely normalize JSON structures (tuples vs lists) by doing json.dumps + json.loads and compare dicts.
