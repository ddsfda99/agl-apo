  - Do not refactor large parts of the codebase to stop using these aliases unless absolutely required to keep tests passing.
  - Do not remove or change `falcon.create_task` / `falcon.get_running_loop` exports unless they are directly part of the `falcon.util` alias deprecation and you add explicit tests for them. (Default: leave top-level exports alone.)
